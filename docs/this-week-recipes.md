## 1. 30 Minute Crispy Cheese Chicken Tinga Tacos

- **Creator:** Half Baked Harvest
- **Cuisine:** Mexican 🌶 (spicy)
- **Total time:** 30 min · **Serves:** 4
- **Recipe link:** https://www.halfbakedharvest.com/crispy-cheese-chicken-tinga-tacos/

_Smoky chipotle shredded chicken crisped into cheesy tacos. Genuinely spicy if you use the full chipotle amount._

**Ingredients**

- 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1 yellow onion
- 2 tbsp chipotle peppers in adobo
- 1 10 oz can red enchilada sauce
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cups shredded Mexican cheese blend
- 10 corn or flour tortillas
- 2 avocado
- 0.5 cup fresh cilantro
- 2 lime
- 1 tsp kosher salt

**Instructions**

1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over high heat. Add chopped chicken and sliced onion; cook, stirring, until browning, ~5 min.
2. Add chipotle peppers, enchilada sauce, oregano, cumin, salt and 1/3 cup water. Reduce to medium and simmer until thick and the chicken shreds easily, ~10 min. Shred the chicken in the sauce.
3. Layer tortillas with cheese and the chicken, fold over, and crisp in a dry skillet (or 425°F oven) until golden and the cheese melts, 3-5 min per side.
4. Top with avocado, cilantro and a squeeze of lime.

## 2. One Pot Marry Me Chicken Pasta

- **Creator:** Half Baked Harvest
- **Cuisine:** Italian
- **Total time:** 35 min · **Serves:** 4
- **Recipe link:** https://www.halfbakedharvest.com/marry-me-chicken-pasta/

_Creamy sun-dried tomato chicken and pasta cooked in one pot with spinach and parmesan._

**Ingredients**

- 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
- 0.5 tsp red pepper flakes
- 4 cloves garlic
- 0.5 cup sun-dried tomatoes
- 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 lb short pasta (penne)
- 2 cups baby spinach
- 1 cup parmesan cheese
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 0.25 cup fresh basil
- 1 tsp kosher salt

**Instructions**

1. Sear seasoned chicken pieces in olive oil in a large pot until golden; remove.
2. Add garlic, sun-dried tomatoes and red pepper flakes; cook 1 min. Add broth and pasta; simmer until pasta is al dente, stirring, ~12 min.
3. Stir in cream, parmesan, spinach and the chicken. Simmer until creamy and chicken is cooked through.
4. Finish with fresh basil.

## 3. 30 Minute Spicy Indian Butter Chicken

- **Creator:** Half Baked Harvest
- **Cuisine:** Indian 🌶 (spicy)
- **Total time:** 30 min · **Serves:** 4
- **Recipe link:** https://www.halfbakedharvest.com/butter-chicken/

_Cozy, creamy one-skillet butter chicken with garam masala, ginger and coconut milk. Bump the cayenne for real heat._

**Ingredients**

- 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 0.5 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cayenne pepper
- 2 tbsp fresh ginger
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 yellow onion
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 15 oz can crushed tomatoes
- 1 14 oz can full-fat coconut milk
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1.5 cups basmati rice
- 4 naan
- 0.25 cup fresh cilantro
- 1 tsp kosher salt

**Instructions**

1. Toss chicken with yogurt, half the garam masala, turmeric and cayenne; let sit 10 min.
2. Melt butter in a skillet; cook onion, ginger and garlic until soft. Add tomato paste and remaining spices, 1 min.
3. Add crushed tomatoes and the chicken; simmer 10 min. Stir in coconut milk and simmer until creamy and chicken is cooked.
4. Season with salt; serve over basmati rice with naan and cilantro.

## 4. Easy Beef and Broccoli (Better Than Takeout)

- **Creator:** Damn Delicious
- **Cuisine:** American Chinese 🌶 (spicy)
- **Total time:** 25 min · **Serves:** 4
- **Recipe link:** https://damndelicious.net/2014/08/13/easy-beef-broccoli/

_Restaurant-style beef and broccoli in ~20 minutes. Sriracha brings the heat._

**Ingredients**

- 1 lb flank steak
- 1 head broccoli
- 0.33 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 0.33 cup low-sodium chicken or beef broth
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp Sriracha
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger
- 1.5 cups jasmine rice
- 2 green onions

**Instructions**

1. Whisk soy sauce, broth, honey, vinegar, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil, cornstarch, Sriracha and ginger into a sauce.
2. Sear thinly sliced steak in a hot skillet until browned; remove.
3. Add broccoli and a splash of water; cook until crisp-tender. Return beef, add sauce, and simmer until glossy and thick.
4. Serve over jasmine rice with green onions.

## 🍨 Dessert. 6 Ingredient Frozen Blackberry Yogurt Bark

- **Creator:** Half Baked Harvest
- **Cuisine:** Dessert
- **Total time:** 15 min · **Serves:** 8
- **Recipe link:** https://www.halfbakedharvest.com/blackberry-yogurt-bark/

_Greek yogurt frozen bark swirled with berry jam — a light, protein-ish freezer treat. ~5 min hands-on._

**Ingredients**

- 2 cups plain Greek yogurt
- 0.33 cup blackberry jam
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- 1 cup fresh blackberries
- 2 oz white chocolate (optional)

**Instructions**

1. Stir yogurt with vanilla and honey; thin slightly with a splash of water. Spread on a parchment-lined sheet.
2. Dollop jam over and swirl. Scatter blackberries; drizzle melted white chocolate if using.
3. Freeze at least 3 hours until firm; break into pieces. Keep frozen.
